=====================================================================
                    TALLY - HOW TO INSTALL (5 to 10 minutes)
=====================================================================

Tally has two parts:

  1. TALLY itself - the app you drop spreadsheets into.
  2. OLLAMA - a free program that runs the AI "brain" on your own
     computer. Tally works without it, but with it you get friendlier
     explanations and can ask questions about your data.

Nothing you upload ever leaves your computer.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 PART 1 - START TALLY
---------------------------------------------------------------------

 ON A MAC
 --------
 Option A (easiest, copy and paste one line):
   1. Open the "Terminal" app (press Cmd + Space, type Terminal, press Enter).
   2. Paste this line and press Enter:

      curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bhg0012/ai-data-analysis-agent/main/install/install-mac.sh | bash

   3. Wait a minute or two the first time. Tally opens by itself, and
      a Tally icon appears on your Desktop for next time.

 Option B (you downloaded the Tally folder as a ZIP):
   1. Unzip it and move the folder somewhere you'll keep it (like Documents).
   2. Double-click "Start Tally (Mac).command".
      If your Mac says it "can't be opened" or "can't be verified":
        - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
        - Scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to the Tally message.
        - Double-click it again and choose "Open".
      You only have to do this once.
   3. After the first start, you can use the "Tally" app icon in the
      same folder (drag it to your Dock if you like).

 ON WINDOWS
 ----------
 Option A (easiest, copy and paste one line):
   1. Click Start, type PowerShell, and open "Windows PowerShell".
   2. Paste this line and press Enter:

      irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bhg0012/ai-data-analysis-agent/main/install/install-windows.ps1 | iex

   3. Wait a minute or two the first time. Tally opens by itself, and
      a Tally shortcut appears on your Desktop for next time.

 Option B (you downloaded the Tally folder as a ZIP):
   1. Right-click the ZIP > "Extract All...", and keep the folder somewhere
      like Documents.
   2. Double-click "Start Tally (Windows).bat".
      If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC":
        click "More info", then "Run anyway". Only needed once.
   3. A Tally shortcut is added to your Desktop. Use that from now on.

 Every time you start Tally, it checks GitHub and updates itself to the
 newest version automatically. A small black window stays open while
 Tally runs. Close it when you're done to quit Tally.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 PART 2 - SET UP THE AI BRAIN (OLLAMA)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

 1. Go to  https://ollama.com  and click "Download".
    Install it like any normal app (Mac: drag to Applications.
    Windows: run the installer).

 2. Open Ollama once.
      Mac: you'll see a small llama icon in the menu bar at the top.
      Windows: you'll see a small llama icon near the clock.
    Ollama now runs quietly in the background. That's all it needs.

 3. In Tally, click the gear icon (top right) to open Settings.
    It should say "Ollama is running, but has no model yet".

 4. Click "Download" next to the recommended model.
    It's a big file (several GB), so it can take 5 to 20 minutes
    depending on your internet. You only do this once.

 5. When it says "Connected", you're done. The dot at the top of Tally
    turns green.

 Which model should I pick?
   - qwen3.5:9b   Recommended. Needs about 16 GB of memory (RAM).
   - gpt-oss:20b  Smartest, but needs about 24 GB of memory.
   - gemma4:e4b   Good writer, works well on most laptops.
   - qwen3.5:4b   For older computers or 8 GB of memory.
   Not sure how much memory you have?
     Mac: Apple menu > About This Mac > "Memory".
     Windows: Start > Settings > System > About > "Installed RAM".

 Prefer typing? You can also download a model in Terminal/PowerShell:
      ollama pull qwen3.5:9b


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
---------------------------------------------------------------------

 "AI not connected" at the top of Tally
   -> Ollama isn't running. Open the Ollama app, then click
      "Check again" in Tally's Settings.

 The AI is very slow
   -> Pick a smaller model in Settings (for example qwen3.5:4b).
      Tally's charts and findings don't need the AI at all; it only
      writes the friendly wording and answers chat questions.

 Ollama is on a different computer or port
   -> Settings > Advanced > "Ollama address" (the default is
      http://localhost:11434).

 Tally won't start at all
   -> Make sure you're connected to the internet the first time
      (it downloads what it needs once). Then try again.
   -> Mac: look at the log in  ~/Library/Logs/Tally.log

 Where are my saved reports?
   Mac:     ~/Library/Application Support/Tally
   Windows: %APPDATA%\Tally
